ARCADE GAME AUSTRALIA.

Arcade and claw machines for events, venues and games rooms across Sydney and NSW. Our work brings together hire, commercial placement, sales, supply and installation.

Arcade Game Australia has five main services: arcade machine hire, claw machine hire, commercial venue placement, arcade machine sales, and amusement machine supply and installation.

If you are planning an event, the date, guests and venue guide the hire enquiry. If you run a pub, club, RSL, shopping centre, cinema or play centre, placement is the starting point for an ongoing games area. Buying a machine is a separate option for home or commercial use.

You can begin with the service and your location. Equipment preferences, a floor plan or photos of the access route can help, but a machine model and a finished layout are not required for the first enquiry.
