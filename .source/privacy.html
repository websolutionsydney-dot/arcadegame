PRIVACY NOTICE.

Information about this website’s enquiry form. Updated 26 September 2026.

THE DETAILS YOU SHARE

When you send an enquiry, this website collects the details you enter: your name, email address, optional phone number and business name, service of interest, location, preferred date and message. Please do not send payment card details, identification documents or sensitive personal information through the form.

Why the information is collected

Enquiry details are used to understand your requirements, respond to questions and discuss a quote or potential booking. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

How an enquiry is stored

The form sends information to this website’s backend, where it is stored in a database for follow-up. Submitted enquiries are not displayed publicly. Website hosting and database providers process information as part of operating the service and may process data outside Australia.

Website connections

Images and fonts are served with the website. Hosting providers may process ordinary server logs needed to operate and protect the website.

Questions, corrections or removal

You can use the contact form to ask about information you previously submitted, request a correction or request its removal. Include your enquiry reference if you have it and the email address used for the original enquiry. Please do not submit identity documents.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website’s enquiry or data-handling features change. The updated notice will be shown on this page.
